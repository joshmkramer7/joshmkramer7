# Joshua Kramer

I build practical operating systems for product leadership: reusable methods that connect customer and market evidence to product decisions, experience design, delivery, launch, growth, operating cadence, and measurable outcomes.

## Purpose

This GitHub portfolio is a working system for making product leadership more coherent, evidence-based, and reusable with responsible AI assistance. The repositories separate portable methods, shared governance, private deployment, and personal experimentation so each can evolve with clear ownership and appropriate controls.

## Business value

The portfolio is designed to help organizations:

- improve the quality and traceability of consequential product decisions;
- connect strategy, customer evidence, design, delivery, launch, and learning;
- reduce rework caused by unclear requirements, ownership, or handoffs;
- turn customer and market signals into accountable action;
- create consistent portfolio visibility without confusing activity with value;
- use AI to support judgment while preserving human decision rights and governance.

## How the portfolio fits together

Product Management OS owns product intent and decisions. Design Lifecycle OS owns human-centered experience work. Customer Listening OS and Market Intelligence OS provide governed evidence. Product Operations OS maintains initiative and portfolio visibility. Product Governance OS defines shared boundaries and handoffs. Company Product OS applies the portfolio inside an approved private environment, while Josh's Product Leadership OS remains the personal experimentation layer.

## Important nuances for business users

- These systems support judgment; they do not replace accountable leaders or approval authorities.
- A generated artifact is not evidence by itself. Claims, assumptions, sources, and decision rights must be verified.
- Each repository has a defined scope. Methods and evidence should stay with their accountable source rather than being copied across systems.
- Portable repositories exclude confidential company context, credentials, customer data, and runtime records.
- Private repositories may not be visible to every visitor even when linked here.
- The portfolio is evolving through versioned pilots; adoption should be proportionate to the risk and reviewed by the relevant owners.

## Reference model

The portfolio uses several recurring principles:

- start with the decision or outcome rather than a preferred artifact;
- preserve evidence provenance, uncertainty, contradictions, and confidence;
- keep one accountable source of truth for each method, decision, and initiative record;
- use the smallest workflow that resolves the current uncertainty;
- separate canonical methods, platform adapters, generated packages, private deployments, and runtime evidence;
- preserve human authority for investment, policy, risk, release, staffing, and commercial commitments.

Each repository README links to its governing contracts, architecture, skill definitions, and operational references.
